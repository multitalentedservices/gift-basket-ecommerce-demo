How it works

Three steps between you and a very good impression

01

Choose or compose

Pick a ready basket from the collections, or build your own — basket, treats and finishing touches, priced live as you go.

02

We pack it by hand

Your basket is composed to order in our Lahore atelier, finished with ribbon, a wax seal and your hand-written card.

03

Delivered, beautifully

Dispatched within 48 hours and delivered nationwide. Free delivery on orders over PKR 10,000.
