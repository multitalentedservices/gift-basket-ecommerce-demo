Matched to the moment

The right basket for this day, not any day.

Jump to your occasion — each section carries the atelier's three best-matched baskets, and the reasoning is written on the card, not hidden in a sales pitch.

Weddings Corporate Birthdays Eid New baby Thank you
Weddings

For the couple, and the photographs

Wedding gifts get displayed, judged and remembered. Go ceremonial: keepsake boxes, sparkling grape, silk and wax seals.

Corporate

Reflects well on the sender

Dignified, brandable, identical at volume. For quantities and branded sleeves, the Corporate page has the full programme.

Birthdays

Tuned to the person, not the calendar

Chocolate people get chocolate towers. Everyone else gets a custom build with their name on the card — start in the composer.

Eid

Dates, done properly

Ajwa and Medjool, stuffed and chocolate-dipped, in crates elders approve of. Order early in Ramadan — the packing bench books out.

New baby

Soft things, and kindness for the parents

Muslin, hand-knitted booties and a rattle for the small person; chocolate and chamomile for the two people who have not slept.

Thank you

Small box, correct priorities

A thank-you should arrive fast and feel considered. These three are our most-sent small gestures.
